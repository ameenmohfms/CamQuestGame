HOW TO PLAY

1 · Pick a mode

Zombies, mummies, aliens or sharks — each with its own world theme.

2 · Point your camera at people

Anyone the camera sees becomes a monster in the game. Move around to hunt!

3 · Tap to shoot

Tap the monster to hit it. Empty clip? Tap the RELOAD button. Headshots near the top score extra.

4 · Respawns

Downed monsters rise again after the respawn timer (change it in Settings).
